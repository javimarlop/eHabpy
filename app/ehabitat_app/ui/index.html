Input variables

The study area and the variables (GeoTIFFs) at two scales: the plot scale (the study area, with the fine pixel size at which the HFTs are mapped) and the ecoregion scale (a coarser pixel size, for the similarity of the HFTs in their ecoregion). They can also be downloaded from the catalog of MonitorEO.

Study areas

a vector file: each ID is a study area where the HFTs are mapped
an integer column; "cat" = one ID per polygon
optional: the names shown with the IDs

Map

study area and preview of the chosen variable

Set of variables

A set is a subfolder of inVars: its GeoTIFFs are the variables of the study area and those of its subfolder ecoregion, the variables of the ecoregion (the RESOLVE ecoregions that the study area touches, or a comparison area of your own). The order of the files is the order of the variables (the first one defines the pixels analysed). Unticked variables go to _excluded, which is not used. A set with only ecoregion is a comparison area for the similarity, not a set for the HFT model.

Plot scale (study area)

    Ecoregion scale

      The study area (the same rectangle as the plot scale) at the pixel and CRS of the ecoregion grid, downloaded because the ecoregion or comparison area does not contain it: the similarity takes the statistics of the HFTs from it, so that they come from the same product and pixel as the area they are compared with.

          Download from MonitorEO

          Variables of the catalog of MonitorEO (OBSNEV, University of Granada) and of any collection or image of Google Earth Engine, computed with the package monitoreo on aligned grids: the study area (with its buffer) and the RESOLVE 2017 ecoregions it touches, or a comparison area of your own. They are saved as a set of variables of inVars. The app keeps monitoreo at its last version of GitHub (when it opens, if it updates itself, and with every download).

          Earth Engine connection

          No account of your own: the research EECUs of each month. Only direct downloads (no exports to Google Drive).
